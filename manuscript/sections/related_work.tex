\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{EEG foundation models and benchmarking.}
REVE uses masked reconstruction over a large, heterogeneous EEG corpus and was
introduced as a representation model that can accommodate arbitrary electrode
arrangements and recording lengths \citep{elouahidi2025reve}. NeuralBench
provides a broader framework for standardized evaluation across neuroimaging
models, datasets, and tasks \citep{banville2026neuralbench}. Our experiment
borrows the optimization budget and age-prediction context but changes the
scientific estimand: the encoder is frozen and only representation heads are
trained. The external MIPDB result must therefore not be compared as though it
were an official NeuralBench leaderboard number.

\paragraph{Representation probing.}
Linear probes were introduced as deliberately restricted readouts of hidden
representations \citep{alain2016probes}. Their restriction is informative: if a
target is linearly decodable, the representation has organized the relevant
signal into a particularly accessible form. Nonlinear or statistics-rich heads
can answer a different question---whether the information can be recovered by
a larger downstream computation. Comparing probes of different capacities is
therefore meaningful only when optimization, data exposure, and evaluation are
matched and when extra expressivity is not silently attributed to the encoder.

\paragraph{EEG age decoding.}
Prior work has shown that age can be predicted from sleep EEG and that derived
brain-age measures capture maturational or health-related variation
\citep{sun2019brainage,vandenbosch2019age}. We do not claim a clinical brain-age
biomarker. Chronological age is used here as a continuous decoding target for
testing the accessibility and external stability of frozen representations.

\paragraph{Nested uncertainty.}
Neuroscience measurements often have multiple levels of variation, and treating
all observations as independent can understate uncertainty. Hierarchical
bootstrap procedures explicitly preserve such structure
\citep{saravanan2020hierarchical}. We resample both optimization seeds and
subjects, then complement that interval with an exact seed-level sign-flip test.
The three candidate hypotheses are adjusted using Holm's sequential procedure
\citep{holm1979simple}.
