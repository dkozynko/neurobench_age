\section{Datasets and Evidence Boundaries}

\paragraph{HBN development data.}
The Healthy Brain Network is a large transdiagnostic pediatric resource with
multimodal measurements, including EEG \citep{alexander2017hbn}. Only the
predeclared HBN train and validation subjects were used to fit heads and select
checkpoints. The repeatedly inspected HBN R5 split was excluded from candidate
selection and is retained solely as retrospective context. Compact source
artifacts available for this report preserve hashes of the HBN subject and
training manifests but intentionally do not reproduce participant-level rows.

\paragraph{MIPDB external data.}
The MIPDB resource includes high-density task-based and task-free EEG for
studying information processing across development \citep{langer2017mipdb}. The
study pinned the NEMAR dataset identity \texttt{nm000153} and content-addressed
the selected acquisition snapshot. A deterministic \PilotSubjects{}-subject
pilot was used only for engineering and target-free signal checks. After
predeclared signal QC, \PrimarySubjects{} subjects within HBN training-age
support formed the primary cohort; \ExtrapolationSubjects{} out-of-support
subjects were reserved for descriptive boundary analyses and were not part of
the confirmatory result.

The cohort flow was also fixed before model evaluation. Of 126 candidate
records, 17 lacked an EEG recording and were excluded before allocation. The
remaining 109 subjects yielded 10 pilot subjects, 79 primary candidates, and
20 extrapolation subjects; four primary candidates then failed the predeclared
signal-QC contract, leaving 75 primary subjects. The HBN training-age support
used for the primary restriction was 5.06--21.67 years. Exceeding the
predeclared minimum of 50 primary subjects is a cohort-size gate, not a formal
power analysis.

\paragraph{Prospective versus secondary evidence.}
No MIPDB primary sample was passed through the study model before the evaluation
start marker. The primary prediction inventory was create-only and analysis was
permitted only after exact completion. In contrast, NeuralBench full fine-tuning
results are secondary reproduction evidence because the encoder is trainable,
and historical HBN R5 comparisons are retrospective because repeated access
eliminates an untouched-holdout interpretation.
The capacity--data and layer-wise extensions also reuse the already evaluated
MIPDB subjects. Their separately locked execution preserves provenance, not
independence from earlier observations; they remain exploratory extensions
rather than additional prospective primary replications.

\paragraph{ds006780 external-transfer cohort.}
OpenNeuro ds006780 is a separate public EEG cohort with 64-channel BioSemi
recordings and age-bearing participant metadata. Under the frozen run-01
contract, 126 subjects had usable ages and signal quality. The cohort includes
typical-development, autism, and sibling groups and differs from MIPDB in
hardware, acquisition context, and cohort composition. We use it as a bounded
cross-cohort transfer test, not as evidence that the encoder never encountered
related data during pretraining. Participant-level metadata and predictions
remain outside the repository; only aggregate statistics and provenance hashes
are retained.
